Project Overview

Context & Research Exploration

An in-depth study of consumer electric vehicle adoption patterns, technological transitions from hybrid to pure battery electric powertrains, and automaker market share.

📈

1. EV Market Growth & Trajectory

Investigating the historical adoption curve from 2011 to 2024, quantifying annual registration increases, and assessing the inflection points driven by policy incentives and consumer sentiment.

  • Annual registration velocity
  • Year-over-year percentage gains
  • Proportion of overall fleet electrification
🔋

2. BEV vs. PHEV Powertrains

Comparative evaluation between pure Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs), charting how consumer preference has progressively tilted toward zero-tailpipe emissions.

  • Battery-only vs hybrid market share
  • Electric range distribution curves
  • Impact of battery pack density gains
🏆

3. Automaker Landscape & Share

Analyzing market concentration across established legacy manufacturers (e.g., Ford, Chevrolet, Nissan, BMW) versus dedicated electric vehicle disruptors (Tesla, Rivian, Lucid).

  • Top 10 manufacturing brands
  • Model-level volume concentration
  • Emergence of premium vs budget tiers
🗺️

4. Geography & Policy (CAFV)

Mapping county and municipality registration density alongside Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle (CAFV) eligibility criteria to examine local adoption clusters.

  • County-level registration densities
  • CAFV rebate qualification breakdown
  • Urban vs rural adoption divergence

Recommended Tableau Story Narrative Chapters:

Chapter 1
Early Market Inception (2011–2016)

Initial EV rollouts dominated by compliance cars and early pioneers (Nissan Leaf, early Tesla Roadster/Model S) with sub-100-mile ranges.

Chapter 2
The Mass Market Tipping Point (2017–2020)

Introduction of high-volume accessible models (Tesla Model 3, Chevy Bolt) shifting consumer perception and sparking fast charging investments.

Chapter 3
Battery Range & Affordability Parity

Scatter analysis correlating battery range improvements with price points, highlighting the decline in battery cost per kWh.

Chapter 4
Electrification Horizon & Legacy Rivals

Legacy automakers (Ford, Hyundai, GM, VW) aggressively entering the market, diversifying segments from sedans to electric SUVs and pickups.

Data Preprocessing & Methodological Integrity

Methodology: Vehicle registration datasets were audited using Python for missing values, range outliers, and duplicate VIN representations. Null values in continuous fields such as Base MSRP and Electric Range were categorized to differentiate between missing data and true zero values (e.g., PHEVs with unrated electric modes).

Architecture Transparency: This academic website serves the analytical models authored in Tableau Public through a clean Python Flask framework. It intentionally does not claim or simulate non-existent SQL/relational database infrastructure, ensuring academic honesty and transparent data pipelines.
